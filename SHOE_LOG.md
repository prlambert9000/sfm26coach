- **Estimated mileage:** 400–600 mi
- **Role at retirement:** Primary daily shoe (incorrect role — should have been race/workout only)
- **Reason retired:** Outsole rubber worn through to foam at heel and forefoot. Foam compression visible in midsole. Past race-shoe lifespan by 2–3x. Carbon-plated super-shoe used as daily trainer is also wrong rotation strategy — replaced by dedicated daily trainer + new race-day pair.
