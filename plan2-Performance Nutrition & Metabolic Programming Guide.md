# **Performance Nutrition & Metabolic Programming Guide**

This guide provides a nutritional framework for a 155 lb (70 kg) athlete with a 58 VO2 Max, focusing on structural repair, inflammatory management, and glycogen optimization for the SF Marathon 2026\.

## **I. Macronutrient Targets for High-Volume Training**

| Nutrient | Daily Target | Strategy |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Protein** | 125g – 140g | Prioritize Eggs, Dairy, and Seafood for Leucine and recovery. |
| **Carbohydrates** | 350g – 500g | Scale up during peak weeks; focus on complex carbs and hydrogels. |
| **Fats** | 60g – 80g | Prioritize Omega-3s (Salmon, Sardines) for joint inflammation. |

## **II. Timing the 6 AM Training Window**

* **Pre-Workout (5:30 AM):** Quick-digesting carbohydrate (half banana, small toast, or 20g carb gel) to top off liver glycogen.  
* **Immediate Post-Workout (7:15 AM):** 30g+ High-Leucine Protein. Example: 4 large eggs or 1.5 cups Greek Yogurt with berries.  
* **Nightly Repair:** 20g Casein-rich protein (Cottage cheese or Milk) before bed to facilitate overnight tissue synthesis.

## **III. The Pescatarian Anti-Inflammatory Protocol**

To manage "Runner's Knee" (patellofemoral pain), dietary intervention targets systemic inflammation:

* **SMASH Fish:** Consume Sardines, Mackerel, Anchovies, Salmon, or Herring 3x per week. High EPA/DHA levels dampen the inflammatory response in the patellar fat pad.  
* **Collagen Co-Factors:** Ensure high Vitamin C intake (Bell peppers, Citrus, Broccoli) alongside protein meals to support tendon health.  
* **Hydration & Electrolytes:** Aim for 1000mg sodium/L during high-sweat 6 AM sessions, utilizing magnesium-rich dairy to prevent cramping.

## **IV. Gut Training for Race Day (90g Carb/Hour)**

The goal is to increase carbohydrate absorption efficiency to reach 90g/hr by Week 10\.

* **Weeks 1-4:** Practice 40g-50g carbs per hour during Sunday long runs.  
* **Weeks 5-8:** Increase to 60g-75g/hr. Utilize hydrogel technology (Maurten) or maple-syrup based concentrates.  
* **Weeks 9-10:** Hit 90g/hr target to simulate race-day intensity and stomach tolerance.

## **V. Metabolic Management for the CEO Schedule**

* **Stress & Cortisol:** High work/construction stress elevates cortisol, which inhibits recovery. Maintain consistent sleep hygiene and prioritize carbohydrate intake on high-stress days to prevent muscle breakdown.  
* **The "Heavy Day" Surplus:** On heavy lifting days (Wednesdays at Cañada College), increase total caloric intake by 300-500 kcal to support muscle protein synthesis.
