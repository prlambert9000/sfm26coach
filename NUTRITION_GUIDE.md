# Performance Nutrition & Metabolic Programming Guide

Pescatarian-aware nutritional framework for a 155 lb (70 kg) athlete with a 58 VO2 max, focused on structural repair, inflammatory management, and glycogen optimization for the SF Marathon 2026.

## I. Macronutrient Targets for High-Volume Training

| Nutrient | Daily Target | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | 125–140 g | Eggs, dairy, seafood for leucine and recovery |
| Carbohydrates | 350–500 g | Scale up during peak weeks; complex carbs and hydrogels |
| Fats | 60–80 g | Omega-3s (salmon, sardines) for joint inflammation |

## II. Timing the 6 AM Training Window

- **Pre-workout (5:30 AM):** Quick-digesting carbohydrate — half banana, small toast, or 20 g carb gel — to top off liver glycogen.
- **Immediate post-workout (7:15 AM):** 30 g+ high-leucine protein. Examples: 4 large eggs, or 1.5 cups Greek yogurt with berries.
- **Nightly repair:** 20 g casein-rich protein (cottage cheese or milk) before bed for overnight tissue synthesis.

## III. Pescatarian Anti-Inflammatory Protocol

To manage patellofemoral inflammation, dietary intervention targets systemic inflammation:

- **SMASH fish 3×/week:** Sardines, mackerel, anchovies, salmon, herring. High EPA/DHA dampens inflammation in the patellar fat pad.
- **Collagen co-factors:** High vitamin C (bell peppers, citrus, broccoli) alongside protein meals to support tendon health.
- **Hydration & electrolytes:** Aim for 1000 mg sodium/L during high-sweat 6 AM sessions. Magnesium-rich dairy to prevent cramping.

## IV. Gut Training for Race Day (90 g Carb/Hour)

Goal: increase carbohydrate absorption efficiency to 90 g/hr by Week 10.

- **Weeks 1–4:** Practice 40–50 g/hr during Sunday long runs.
- **Weeks 5–8:** Increase to 60–75 g/hr. Hydrogel technology (Maurten) or maple-syrup-based concentrates.
- **Weeks 9–10:** Hit 90 g/hr to simulate race-day intensity and stomach tolerance.

### Bay to Breakers fuel rehearsal (Week 2 — first real practice)

B2B (May 17) is the first race-pace effort long enough to need real fueling. The Thursday progression run before it is the dry run — same protocol, smaller dose, threshold finish to stress the gut.

- **Thu May 14 (6 mi progression):** Pre-run 25 g carb (banana + ½ slice toast OR 1 Maurten Gel 100) + 8 oz water with electrolyte 30 min out. **One gel (~25 g) at mile 3.5,** right before the threshold transition. Note any GI complaints — this informs the Sunday gel choice.
- **Sat May 16 (5K shakeout):** No fuel. Normal hydration only.
- **Sun May 17 (B2B 15K, 10 mi total):** 30 g carb 60 min pre-start + electrolyte sip. **Gel at mile 3, gel at mile 6** (right before MP transition) = ~50 g across ~80 min ≈ 40 g/hr. Water at every aid station.

Default gel: Maurten Gel 100 (25 g carb, no flavoring artifacts on stomach). Backup: SiS Beta Fuel or Gu Roctane.

## V. Metabolic Management for the CEO Schedule

- **Stress & cortisol:** High work/construction stress elevates cortisol, which inhibits recovery. Maintain consistent sleep hygiene and prioritize carbohydrate intake on high-stress days to prevent muscle breakdown.
- **Heavy day surplus:** On heavy lifting days (Wednesdays at Cañada College), increase total caloric intake by 300–500 kcal to support muscle protein synthesis.
