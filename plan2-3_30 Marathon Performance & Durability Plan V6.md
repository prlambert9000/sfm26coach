# **3:30 Marathon Performance & Durability Plan V6**

This 12-week periodized plan bridges the gap between a high aerobic capacity (VO2 Max 58\) and the structural durability required for a strong finish at the San Francisco Marathon on July 26, 2026\. The strategy prioritizes a "slow ramp" starting May 4, 2026, with smoothed deload weeks and explicit protective measures for patellar tendon health.

## **I. Pace Targets & Performance Metrics**

| Workout Type | Target Pace / Effort | Purpose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Marathon Pace (MP)** | 7:55 – 8:00 min/mile | 3:30 Baseline Goal |
| **Threshold / Intervals** | 7:15 – 7:25 min/mile | Aerobic Ceiling Elevation |
| **Easy / Recovery** | 9:00 – 9:45 min/mile | Capillary Density & Recovery |
| **Long Run (Steady)** | 8:45 – 9:15 min/mile | Fat Oxidation & Time on Feet |

## **II. Weekly Training Structure (65-Minute Windows)**

* **Pre-Run Activation (All Days):** 5-minute routine (Hip CARs, Band Walks, Glute Bridges).  
* **Monday:** 20-minute hip complex circuit (90/90 switches, adductor rockbacks, foam rolling).  
* **Tuesday (6 AM):** **Track Intervals.** 1.5-mile easy jog to the high school track, progressing interval distances, 1.5-mile easy jog back.  
* **Wednesday (6 AM):** **Heavy Strength Foundation.** Back Squat, RDL, Reverse Nordics, Standing Calf Raises (Cañada College).  
* **Thursday (6 AM):** **Alternating Base/Progression.** Odd weeks: Easy base. Even weeks: Progression finish (last 2 miles @ 8:15).  
* **Friday (6 AM):** **Stability & Power.** Bulgarian Split Squats, Single-Leg Step-ups, Spanish Squat holds, Copenhagen Planks (Cañada College).  
* **Saturday:** Shakeout Run (3-4 miles) or Rest.  
* **Sunday:** **The Long Run.** Smoothed progression with explicit 90g/hr gut training rehearsals.

## **III. The Escape Valve (Zero-Impact Rule)**

If knee status hits a 3 or higher on a 5-point pain scale (where 1 \= no awareness, 5 \= pain that alters gait) upon waking, automatically substitute any easy or steady run for an equivalent time/heart rate session on a stationary bike or elliptical.

## **IV. The "Week 7 Pivot" Strategy**

The Phase 1 & 2 targets are set for a conservative 3:30 finish. The Week 7 Presidio Half Marathon Benchmark will serve as an official reassessment. To upgrade targets for a 3:20-3:25 finish, the following objective criteria must be met:

* Sub-1:44 finish time.  
* Average HR under 170 BPM in the final 3 miles.  
* Zero knee compensation crossing the finish line.
