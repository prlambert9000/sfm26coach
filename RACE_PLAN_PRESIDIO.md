# Race Plan — Presidio Half Marathon (June 21, 2026)

**Week 7 Pivot Test.** Course: Crissy Field start → climb to the Golden Gate Bridge → out-and-back on the bridge → back through the Presidio → finish at Crissy Field. ~60–90 ft/mi, modestly hillier per mile than the SF Marathon course.

> Paces below are **effort-shaped targets**, not surveyed per-mile elevation. On the climbs, run the *effort* and let pace float up — don't force the number. Surveyed elevation not yet pinned; revise if a course profile is obtained.

## The three gates (priority order — all three required to upgrade 3:30 → 3:25)

1. **Sub-1:37:30 finish.** The pacing above delivers this with cushion.
2. **Avg HR <170 in the final 3 miles.** This is why the press is *gradual*. Glance at HR through miles 11–13 — if it climbs past ~169, hold, don't push. Threshold reads ~162–165, so a hard-but-controlled HM finish should live in the high-160s — achievable, but a real constraint. (Number may shift if a max-HR test lands a lower max before race day.)
3. **Zero knee compensation across the line.** On every descent (off the bridge, back to Crissy): **shorten stride, quicken cadence, don't bomb it.** Downhill eccentric load is the patellofemoral joint's least-favorite stimulus.

Miss **any one** → hold the 3:30 targets.

## Execution principles

- **Negative-split by effort, not by forcing pace.** First 6 miles bank patience; miles 10–13 spend it — *if* HR and knee say yes.
- **Hills are run by effort.** The 7:40 / 7:35 climb miles are features, not failures — make the time back on the runnable bridge and descents.
- **Abort the time chase if needed.** If at mile 10 the HR is pinned or the knee's talking, hold a steady effort and finish clean. A blown HR gate or a knee tweak fails the pivot just as surely as a slow clock — **the knee outranks everything.**
- **Opportunistic max-HR capture:** a hard HM finish naturally approaches max. If the close is genuinely maximal and the trace is clean, the peak (incl. ~30s post-finish) is a candidate true-max read — log it, but only if the effort was actually maximal.
